### 1. Objective of Proposed Partnership
Sahaay AI invites [Partner Organization] to collaborate on a groundbreaking public-safety initiative: building an AI early-warning platform to predict and detect severe street waterlogging across Hyderabad during monsoon storms.

By contributing front-facing forward dashcam footage captured during routine commercial deliveries and transit trips along major Hyderabad corridors, your organization will directly contribute to life-saving urban flood intelligence while helping optimize route passability for your own drivers and fleet assets.

### 2. Scope of Imagery Requested
1. **Target Corridors**: Arterial and low-lying transit corridors across Hyderabad (e.g., Gachibowli–Madhapur IT corridor, Begumpet–Secunderabad arterial, Tolichowki–Mehdipatnam link).
2. **Data Modality**: Short front-facing forward-facing camera video clips or periodic frame extractions (1 frame every 5–10 seconds) recorded during daylight or nighttime rainfall events.
3. **Excluded Imagery**: **Cabin-facing interior video is strictly excluded.** Zero audio recordings, passenger faces, or driver biometric data will ever be collected or stored.

### 3. Privacy, Confidentiality, and Anonymization Guarantees
1. **Automated License Plate & Face Redaction**: Every frame ingested into our pipeline is immediately scrubbed using heavy Gaussian blurring on all vehicle plates and pedestrian faces.
2. **Route Confidentiality**: Raw trip trajectories and commercial delivery addresses will be coarsened to corridor-level road segments (radius $\approx 500\text{ m}$). Commercial stop manifests and customer identities are never ingested.
3. **Vault Security**: All raw partner video is stored in restricted, AES-256 encrypted storage accessible only to vetted data compliance officers.

### 4. Permitted Use & Machine Learning Rights
- Ingesting de-identified frames into Sahaay AI training and evaluation manifests.
- Training open-access computer vision models to classify flood severity and passability.
- Strictly prohibited: Commercial resale of partner imagery, driver performance monitoring, or regulatory dispute reporting.

### 5. Mutual Benefits for Fleet Partner
- **Complimentary Real-Time Flood Intelligence API**: Priority API access to Sahaay AI real-time flood severity alerts and passability assessments for your dispatch routing.
- **Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Leadership**: Public recognition as a foundational technology partner in Hyderabad's urban climate resilience initiative.
