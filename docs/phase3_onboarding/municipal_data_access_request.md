### 1. Project Background and Objective
Sahaay AI is an AI-powered urban flood intelligence and early-warning research platform designed specifically for the Greater Hyderabad metropolitan area. The platform synthesizes real-time rainfall data, terrain vulnerability topography, and street-level imagery to provide advisory alerts to municipal disaster response crews and citizens.

To train and validate robust computer vision models capable of distinguishing severe street inundation from normal wet roads, puddles, and surface reflections, Sahaay AI requires authentic ground-level optical data from flood-vulnerable urban corridors in Hyderabad.

### 2. Scope of Data Requested
We respectfully request scheduled or event-driven image snapshot access to designated traffic and municipal CCTV cameras covering verified waterlogging hotspots:
1. **Target Locations (Pilot Phase)**:
   - Tolichowki Flyover Underpass (`HYD-LOC-001`)
   - Shaikpet Nala Road (`HYD-LOC-002`)
   - Begumpet Rasoolpura Underpass (`HYD-LOC-003`)
   - Panjagutta Underpass (`HYD-LOC-006`)
2. **Data Format**: Periodic static image snapshots (JPEG/PNG) captured at 5- to 15-minute intervals during active rainfall events and dry baseline periods. Continuous raw video streaming is NOT required.
3. **Proposed Duration**: Initial 6-month pilot covering the monsoon and post-monsoon evaluation cycles.

### 3. Machine Learning Training and Permitted Use
We explicitly request authorization to:
- Ingest image snapshots into the Sahaay AI research data pipeline.
- Annotate images according to standardized visual water-extent categories.
- Utilize anonymized images to benchmark, train, and evaluate open-access convolutional and vision-transformer flood classification models.
- Publish aggregated academic benchmarks without disclosing raw unredacted imagery.
- Strictly prohibited use: Commercial resale, surveillance of individuals, facial recognition, or traffic violation enforcement.

### 4. Privacy, Redaction, and Security Safeguards
1. **Automated De-Identification**: All received snapshots will be immediately processed through automated Gaussian blurring filters targeting all human faces and vehicle registration plates.
2. **Encrypted Storage**: Raw imagery will be stored in an access-controlled, AES-256 encrypted compliance vault accessible only to cleared research compliance leads.
3. **Retention & Purging**: Ingested imagery will be retained for a maximum of 36 months, after which assets will be securely purged in accordance with municipal guidelines.
