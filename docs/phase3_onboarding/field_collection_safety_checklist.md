# DRAFT: Field-Team Collection Safety Protocol & Pre-Dispatch Checklist

> [!CRITICAL]
> **SAFETY FIRST MANDATE**:
> No photograph or data point is worth human injury or distress. Field teams are strictly forbidden from taking risks, entering floodwaters, or stepping into active traffic.

### 1. Mandatory Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Checklist
Every field researcher deployed for ground collection must be equipped with:
- [ ] **High-Visibility Safety Vest**: Class 2 or Class 3 reflective vest worn over all outerwear at all times.
- [ ] **Non-Slip Footwear**: Closed-toe waterproof boots with heavy slip-resistant rubber tread. Sandals, flip-flops, or open shoes are strictly prohibited.
- [ ] **Waterproof Gear Enclosure**: Sealed IPX7 waterproof pouches or cases for camera and mobile devices.
- [ ] **Charged Cellular Phone**: Smartphone with minimum 80% battery charge and power bank.
- [ ] **Emergency Contact Speed Dial**: Programmed with GHMC Disaster Control (`040-21111111`), Police Control (`100`), Ambulance (`108`), and the Field Operations Lead.
- [ ] **Personal First Aid Kit**: Basic antiseptic wipes, waterproof bandages, and oral rehydration salts.

---

### 2. Pre-Dispatch Site Verification Checklist
Before departing for any candidate corridor:
- [ ] **IMD Weather Alert Check**: Confirm IMD Hyderabad bulletin is NOT a Red Alert (extremely heavy rain with squally winds). If Red Alert is active, **cancel field deployment immediately**.
- [ ] **Designated Safe Vantage Verification**: Confirm the location features a verified, elevated pedestrian sidewalk or foot overbridge at least 1.5 meters above maximum anticipated storm surge.
- [ ] **Buddy System Confirmation**: Field researchers must operate in pairs (minimum 2 persons). Solo deployment is strictly prohibited.
- [ ] **Operations Desk Check-In**: Log planned departure, corridor destination, and expected return time with the operations desk.

---

### 3. On-Site Operational Rules & Absolute Prohibitions
While at the collection location:
- [ ] **Zero Water Entry**: **NEVER step into flowing water, standing floodwater, or submerged drains.** Hidden open manholes, displaced drain grates, and submerged debris present fatal hazards.
- [ ] **Stay Out of Traffic**: Remain strictly on designated pedestrian walkways or curbs. **NEVER step into active vehicular lanes.**
- [ ] **Stable Stance**: Do not lean over bridge parapets, climb utility poles, or stand on muddy, unpaved nala embankments prone to collapse.
- [ ] **Emergency Priority**: If emergency vehicles (fire, NDRF, police, GHMC monsoon teams) approach, immediately clear the area. Never obstruct, distract, or crowd first responders.
- [ ] **Law Enforcement Courtesy**: If questioned by Hyderabad Traffic Police or GHMC officials, immediately present official university/institution photo ID and research authorization letter. Comply politely with any request to vacate.

---

### 4. Immediate Abort & Evacuation Triggers
Cease collection and evacuate to high, covered ground immediately if:
1. **Lightning / Thunderstorm**: Thunder is heard or lightning is observed within 3 km (flash-to-bang interval $< 10$ seconds).
2. **Surging Floodwaters**: Water rapidly rises onto pedestrian walkways or overtops the curb.
3. **Severe Wind**: Wind gusts exceed 40 km/h with risk of falling tree branches or hoardings.
4. **Any Team Member Discomfort**: Fatigue, injury, or equipment compromise.
