**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Mandatory Operational Standard  
**Date:** 2026-10-25  
**Scope:** Human-Supervised Ground-Truth Observation & Environmental Validation in Hyderabad

## 1. Cardinal Safety Directives (Zero-Tolerance Rules)

Under no circumstances may any field team member, researcher, or contractor violate the following rules:

> [!CAUTION]
> **RULE 1: NEVER ENTER FLOODWATER.**  
> No researcher may step, wade, drive, or walk into standing, rising, or flowing water of any depth. Submerged open manholes, displaced sewer covers, electrified downed wires, and hidden debris present mortal hazards.

> [!CAUTION]
> **RULE 2: NEVER STAND IN OR ADJACENT TO MOVING TRAFFIC.**  
> Observations must be conducted exclusively from permanent, elevated, physical pedestrian barriers (e.g. foot over-bridges, wide pedestrian plazas, municipal building balconies). Standing in carriage lanes, roadway shoulders, or median dividers is strictly prohibited.

> [!CAUTION]
> **RULE 3: NEVER STAGE OR MANUFACTURE FLOODING.**  
> Artificial inundation, deliberate drain blockages, or simulated road hazards are strictly prohibited. All observations must capture naturally occurring environmental conditions.

> [!CAUTION]
> **RULE 4: NEVER ACCESS RESTRICTED MUNICIPAL INFRASTRUCTURE.**  
> Climbing onto stormwater canal retaining walls, utility poles, high-voltage substations, or closed government perimeters without written authorization is strictly prohibited.

## 3. Required Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) & Gear

Before departing base camp, every team member must be outfitted with:

1. **High-Visibility Outerwear:** EN ISO 20471 / IS 15809 Class 2 fluorescent yellow/orange safety vests with reflective prismatic tape.
2. **Waterproof Footwear:** Heavy-duty, slip-resistant, steel-shank rubberized work boots (EN ISO 20345 / IS 15298 compliant).
3. **Monsoon Foul-Weather Gear:** High-visibility breathable waterproof rainsuits (jacket with hood and trousers).
4. **Protective Headgear:** Industrial safety helmet with chin strap.
5. **Calibrated Non-Contact Measurement Tools:**
   - Handheld optical laser rangefinder with inclinometer.
   - High-magnification optical monocular/binoculars with reticle for reading physical curb markings and painted bridge staff gauges remotely.
   - Long-reach telescopic fiberglass depth staff ($3\text{ m}$) — **to be used ONLY from dry pedestrian footbridges where vertical access is protected by structural railings.**
6. **Communication & Life Safety Pack:**
   - Dedicated cellular satellite/GSM two-way communication device with offline GPS.
   - Fully charged portable power banks ($20,000\text{ mAh}$).
   - Comprehensive trauma first-aid kit (including antiseptic wash, sterile bandages, tourniquet, snakebite compression kit, burn gel).
   - High-intensity waterproof LED flashlights ($1,000\text{ lumens}$) and safety whistles.

## 4. Operational Boundaries: Daylight & Weather Restrictions

1. **Daylight Operations Only:** All field deployments must occur between **08:00 IST and 17:30 IST**. Zero nighttime, dawn, or dusk deployments are permitted during the pilot phase due to severely compromised visibility and heightened electrocution risks.
2. **Lightning & Thunderstorm Thresholds:**
   - If thunder is heard or lightning is detected within $10\text{ km}$ via the India Meteorological Department (IMD) / Damini app, the deployment is placed on **STANDBY**.
   - If lightning occurs within $5\text{ km}$, the team must immediately abort and shelter inside an enclosed, grounded concrete building or vehicle.
3. **Severe Wind & Cyclone Thresholds:**
   - Sustained wind speeds exceeding $35\text{ km/h}$ or gusts exceeding $50\text{ km/h}$ require immediate cancellation due to falling trees, hoardings, and loose tin roofing.

## 5. Abort Triggers & Immediate Retreat Protocol

The Field Lead or Safety Officer must order an **IMMEDIATE ABORT** if any of the following conditions occur:

| Trigger Code | Hazard Condition | Mandatory Action |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **ABORT-01** | Water rising toward the designated pedestrian vantage point ($\le 30\text{ cm}$ vertical freeboard). | Evacuate immediately to pre-designated higher ground. |
| **ABORT-02** | Electric sparking, downed utility wires, or transformer humming detected in vicinity. | Evacuate immediately; alert TSSPDCL (1912) and GHMC (040-21111111). |
| **ABORT-03** | Structural vibration, cracking, or railing instability on foot over-bridge. | Vacate the structure immediately. |
| **ABORT-04** | Road traffic gridlock causing aggressive vehicle diversion onto pedestrian sidewalks. | Retreat into an adjacent commercial building lobby. |
| **ABORT-05** | Public hostility, crowd gathering, or interference with equipment. | Pack gear calmly, depart the area, and notify local police. |
| **ABORT-06** | Loss of cellular/emergency communications for $> 15\text{ minutes}$. | Abort deployment and return to base camp. |
